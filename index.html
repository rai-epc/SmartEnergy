What is an Energy Performance Certificate (EPC)?

An Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) rates how energy efficient your building is using grades from A (very efficient) to G (inefficient). It tells you how costly it will be to heat and light your property, and what its carbon dioxide emissions are likely to be.

EPCs are legally required when a property is built, sold, or rented. The certificate also includes recommendations on ways to improve your home's energy efficiency to save you money and help the environment.

What is a Retrofit Assessment?

A Retrofit Assessment is a comprehensive evaluation of your home to identify suitable energy efficiency improvements. It goes beyond a standard EPC by considering the building as a whole system (fabric, services, ventilation) and how different measures interact.

This assessment is crucial for planning effective retrofit projects (like installing insulation, new heating systems, or renewable energy sources) ensuring they deliver the intended benefits without causing unintended consequences (like damp or poor air quality). It's often the first step in accessing funding or grants for home energy upgrades.

How We Carry Out Assessments

Our qualified assessors conduct a thorough survey of your property. This involves:

  • Inspecting lofts, walls, floors, windows, and doors.
  • Examining your heating, hot water, and ventilation systems.
  • Taking measurements and photographs.
  • Gathering information about the property's construction and age.

For EPCs, we use accredited software (RdSAP or SBEM) to calculate the energy rating. For Retrofit Assessments, we perform a more detailed analysis considering occupant behaviour, ventilation needs, and potential risks, following PAS 2035 standards where applicable.

The Purpose of Energy Assessments

The primary goals of EPC and Retrofit Assessments are to:

  • Inform Homeowners: Provide clear information about a property's energy usage and potential running costs.
  • Identify Improvements: Recommend cost-effective measures to enhance energy efficiency.
  • Reduce Energy Bills: Help lower heating and electricity costs through targeted upgrades.
  • Lower Carbon Footprint: Contribute to environmental protection by reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
  • Increase Comfort: Improve living conditions by creating warmer, more comfortable homes.
  • Comply with Regulations: Meet legal requirements for property transactions and access potential grants/funding.
